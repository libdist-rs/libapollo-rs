Note on latency
---------------
The reported `Avg Latency` is end-to-end from the moment the client fires a
transaction until the client observes the containing block. Because the test
keeps 10 000 tx in flight, latency is dominated by queueing
(a tx waits ~`window / throughput` seconds before being batched into a block).
To measure raw commit latency, rerun with `-w 1` or a window smaller than one block.
